Before you play

Know the basics.

  • This is a drinking game. Know your limits and drink responsibly.
  • The game begins with nine face-up cards arranged in a 3×3 grid.
  • Aces are low.
  • Drawing a joker means you drink.
Your goal

Survive each turn, consolidate the table down to one final stack, and play through the entire last deck.

playing_cards

Your turn

Choose. Predict. Stack.

  1. 1

    Choose a card

    Select one of the face-up cards currently on the table.

  2. 2

    Guess higher or lower

    Predict whether the next card from the deck will be higher or lower than the selected card.

  3. 3

    Reveal and stack

    Draw the next card and place it face-up on the chosen stack.

  4. 4

    Get three correct

    Three correct predictions end your turn. They do not need to be consecutive.

Assigned time

The number of cards in the chosen stack determines how long you drink.

The table controls the timer.

While you drink, every other player can repeatedly tap their screen to slow the countdown and increase your actual drinking time.

The endgame

Every reshuffle makes it harder.

1

The deck runs out.

2

One row of stacks is consolidated.

3

Those cards are shuffled into the new deck.

4

Repeat until only one stack remains.

K♥

One stack left

This is where it gets brutal.

Everyone must keep guessing against the same growing stack. Wrong guesses become more common and the drinking time keeps increasing.

The game ends after the entire final deck has been played onto that last stack.
